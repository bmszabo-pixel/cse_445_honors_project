# Northwind Gear - Store Policies & Customer Guide

## 1. Return & Exchange Policy
- **Return Window**: Customers may return any eligible product within **30 days** of delivery.
- **Condition**: Items must be in new, unused condition with original tags, packaging, and proof of purchase (receipt or order number).
- **Return Shipping**: Return shipping is **100% free for item exchanges** or store credit. For refunds back to the original payment method, a flat return shipping fee of **$5.99** is deducted from the refund total.
- **Non-Returnable Items**: Water filter cartridges that have been opened/wetted, fuel canisters, and final clearance items marked "Final Sale".

## 2. Shipping & Delivery Policy
- **Free Shipping Threshold**: We offer **Free Standard Shipping on all orders over $50** within the continental United States.
- **Standard Shipping (Orders under $50)**: A flat shipping rate of **$6.95** applies to orders under $50. Standard delivery typically takes **3 to 5 business days**.
- **Expedited Shipping**: 2-Day Expedited Shipping is available at checkout for **$14.99**.
- **Order Processing**: Orders placed before 1:00 PM MST on business days ship the same day. Orders placed on weekends or holidays begin processing the next business day.
- **Tracking**: Tracking numbers are generated and emailed once your carrier picks up your package.

## 3. Warranty & Repairs
- **1-Year Manufacturer Warranty**: Every Northwind Gear product is backed by our comprehensive **1-Year Limited Manufacturer Warranty**.
- **Coverage**: Covers all defects in materials and manufacturing craftsmanship (e.g., broken seams, defective zippers, faulty pole ferrules, delaminating materials).
- **Exclusions**: Does not cover normal wear and tear, cosmetic scuffs, punctures or tears from sharp rocks/crampons, accidental misuse, or improper gear washing.
- **Warranty Claims**: Contact support@northwindgear.com with your order ID, a description of the defect, and photos of the issue.

## 4. Price Match Guarantee
- We match prices from authorized outdoor gear retailers on identical, in-stock products within 14 days of your purchase.
